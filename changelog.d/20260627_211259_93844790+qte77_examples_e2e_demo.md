### Added

- `examples/` — a runnable end-to-end demo (`make demo`) over a small bundled corpus: two public-domain text excerpts (RFC 2822, a Lincoln letter) plus a synthetic record that exercises every detector (structured + opt-in PHI + EU IDs, all checksum-valid). The demo uses an ephemeral key and writes to a gitignored `examples/_out/` — no key/mapping/plaintext committed. `examples/README.md` cross-references the larger [doc-pipeline-engine](https://github.com/qte77/doc-pipeline-engine) sample corpus.
