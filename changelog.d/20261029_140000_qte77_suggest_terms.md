### Added

- `pseudonymize suggest-terms <in_dir> [--terms FILE] [--out PATH]`: a dry-run that runs NER (`[ner]` extra) and emits `terms.csv`-ready candidate rows (`value,type,id,count`). Candidates are deduped case-insensitively, ranked by frequency, and skipped when an existing term already covers them. It needs no key and writes no output, mapping, or report. (#106)
