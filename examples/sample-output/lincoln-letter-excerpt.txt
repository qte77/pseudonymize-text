<LOC:0347e67c46abbba9b1d7112913bbd34f>, Dec. 24th, 1848.

My dear father:--

Your letter of the 7th was received night before last. I very
cheerfully send you the twenty dollars, which sum you say is
necessary to save your land from sale. Give my love to Mother,
and all the connections.

Affectionately your son,

<NAME:74a0c6bc3ea7f7b518d39c46476b2a3d>.
